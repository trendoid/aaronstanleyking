# The Cloud Age

In human existence there have been several archaeological periods. Humans use these terms to classify segments of time. They are not very distinct but show differences and can be used to trend human capabilities and accomplishments. I believe humans have ventured into another such period in our evolution or de-evolution. I'm not going to judge on comment on whether this is a step forward in our enlightenment or a step farther into an abyss.

As you have read from the title of this writing, I believe humans are entering the Cloud Age. Before I tell you about the Cloud Age, let's look back. This brief reference might help put into context my hypothesis. Some or all of these previous classifications are widely regarded as the ages of man.

## Previous Ages

**Stone Age** – The period in time we humans just sat around enjoying the view, trying to stay alive, keep warm and procreate. If you wanted to make a tool because you couldn't stay alive, warm or procreate well enough with your hands, you had to go find a rock and chip away at it until it could help you accomplish your goal. You owned your rock. If you lost it, broke it, or had it stolen you had to make another one. You were responsible for the whole process from raw material to finished good.

**Bronze Age** – The period in time when humans discovered that certain combinations of materials could make tools that were better than stone tools. You could make something stronger, sharper, and longer lasting. The process was more complex than making stone tools. You had to mine, melt, mix, and mold. This made the tools less commonly owned. If you wanted a bronze tool you might have to trade for it rather than make it yourself.

**Iron Age** – Similar to the Bronze Age but with better materials and more complex processes. Even fewer people could master the complete process of creating these tools.

## The Cloud Age

![Cloud computing and modern technology](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1451187580459-43490279c0fa?ixlib=rb-4.0.3&ixid=M3wxMjA3fDB8MHxwaG90by1wYWdlfHx8fGVufDB8fHx8fA%3D%3D&auto=format&fit=crop&w=2072&q=80)

We're living in the cloud age of computing. Everything is connected, everything is online, and the possibilities are endless.

### Characteristics of the Cloud Age

1. **Location Independence**: Your tools and data exist somewhere, but you don't know or care where
2. **Shared Infrastructure**: Multiple people use the same underlying resources
3. **Service-Based Access**: You consume capabilities rather than own physical tools
4. **Dependency on Connectivity**: Without a connection, your tools become inaccessible

### The Trade-offs

Just as in previous ages, we're trading ownership and control for convenience and capability. In the Stone Age, you owned your rock completely. In the Cloud Age, you own... well, very little actually. Your photos are on someone else's servers, your applications run on someone else's infrastructure, your documents are stored in someone else's data centers.

### What This Means

Whether this is progress or regress depends on your perspective. We gain:
- Access to incredibly powerful tools
- Global collaboration capabilities  
- Automatic updates and maintenance
- Scalability we could never achieve alone

But we lose:
- Direct ownership and control
- Independence from service providers
- Privacy and data sovereignty
- The ability to function when disconnected

## Conclusion

Like all previous ages, the Cloud Age represents both advancement and trade-offs. The question isn't whether it's good or bad - it's how we adapt to thrive in this new reality while being aware of what we're giving up in the process.

The Cloud Age is here. The question is: are we ready for it?
