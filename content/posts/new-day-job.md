# New Day Job

![Career growth and new opportunities](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1507003211169-0a1dd7228f2d?ixlib=rb-4.0.3&ixid=M3wxMjA3fDB8MHxwaG90by1wYWdlfHx8fGVufDB8fHx8fA%3D%3D&auto=format&fit=crop&w=2070&q=80)

I'm excited to share that I've started a new position as a Senior Software Engineer. It's been an amazing journey getting here, and I'm looking forward to the challenges and opportunities ahead.---

*This post reflects an important theme that would recur throughout Aaron's career - the balance between professional development and family life. Even early in his blogging journey, he recognized that stepping away from coding to spend time with family wasn't a professional failure, but a conscious choice about what matters most.*
