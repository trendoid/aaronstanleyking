# User Groups in Indiana are the Best!

![Developer community and networking events](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1515187029135-18ee286d815b?ixlib=rb-4.0.3&ixid=M3wxMjA3fDB8MHxwaG90by1wYWdlfHx8fGVufDB8fHx8fA%3D%3D&auto=format&fit=crop&w=2070&q=80)

Many people know that I'm really into (addicted) to user groups and technical conferences. I love monthly meetings, code and coffees, meet-ups, happy hours, and even the big conferences. The reason is that I love what I do for a living. It's work just like all jobs. I don't like HR crap, contracts, financials, and other office duties. But I love to code. I love research and development. So I really like to talk about what I do. I love what I do for a living.

## My 2011 Focus

In the year 2011, I'll be speaking in Indiana. I'm not flying out to NYC, Kansas City, or driving up to Michigan or down to Kentucky. I'm going to try to talk to the fellow Hoosiers and build the community here. It's not that I won't go out of state if asked, but I'm going to try to not submit abstracts and focus on Indiana.

## Current Schedule

Here is the current schedule:

**January 13th** - Indianapolis .NET Developers Association – ASP.NET MVC 3  
**March 2nd** - Fort Wayne .NET Users Group – ASP.NET MVC 3  
**March 10th** - Evansville .NET Users Group – ASP.NET MVC 3

## Why Focus Local?

There are several reasons I'm making this commitment:

1. **Community Building**: Indiana has great developer talent, but sometimes we don't invest enough in our local communities
2. **Relationship Building**: Deeper relationships form when you consistently show up for the same groups
3. **Knowledge Sharing**: I want to make sure Hoosier developers have access to the same quality content available in larger tech hubs
4. **Personal Growth**: Teaching the same topic to different audiences helps me refine and improve my presentations

## The Indiana Developer Scene

Indiana has an incredible developer community that doesn't always get the recognition it deserves. We have:

- Strong .NET user groups across the state
- Passionate developers working on cutting-edge projects
- Great companies building innovative solutions
- A collaborative spirit that makes everyone better

## ASP.NET MVC 3 Focus

I'm particularly excited to be talking about ASP.NET MVC 3 since I've been using MVC in production almost since its inception. The improvements in version 3, especially with the Razor view engine, are game-changing for web development productivity.

## Looking Forward

While I'm focusing locally this year, I hope this investment in Indiana's developer community pays dividends for everyone involved. Strong local communities make the entire industry stronger.

If you're organizing a user group in Indiana and want to discuss having me speak, please reach out. Let's make 2011 a great year for Indiana developers!
