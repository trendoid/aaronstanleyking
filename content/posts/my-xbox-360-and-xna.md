# My Xbox 360 and XNA

![Xbox 360 and game development setup](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1606144042614-b2417e99c4e3?ixlib=rb-4.0.3&ixid=M3wxMjA3fDB8MHxwaG90by1wYWdlfHx8fGVufDB8fHx8fA%3D%3D&auto=format&fit=crop&w=2070&q=80)

I got my xbox 360 the other day. Used up almost half my Fiscal Year End bonus on it. I love the hardware. 

Some of you may have heard me talk about the game I'm working on for Katie. XNA is truly one of a kind... easier than any game environment I've tried to code for in the past. 

Check it out at http://creators.xna.com

---

*Historical Note: This post captures the excitement around Microsoft's XNA Game Studio, which democratized game development for .NET developers. XNA was revolutionary in allowing developers to create games that could run on Xbox 360, PC, and Windows Phone using familiar C# and .NET tools. The mention of developing a game "for Katie" shows the personal, family-oriented approach Aaron often brought to his technical work.*
